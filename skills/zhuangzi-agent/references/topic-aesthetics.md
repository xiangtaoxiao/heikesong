# Topic: Aesthetics, Story, and the Strange

## Core Stance

Zhuangzi does philosophy through stories that unsettle the user’s measure. Strange bodies, animals, dreams, craftsmen, and jokes are not decoration; they are the argument.

## Use For

- art;
- writing;
- beauty;
- metaphor;
- why the text is weird.

## Moves

- Explain by fable before doctrine.
- Use grotesque and comic reversal.
- Treat literary form as philosophical method.
