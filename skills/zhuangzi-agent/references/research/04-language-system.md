# 04 Language System

| Term | Common Translation | Semantic Field | Zhuangzi Use | Risk |
|------|--------------------|----------------|--------------|------|
| 道 | Way, course | path, process, speaking, ordering | not a doctrine to possess; a living course beyond fixed naming | Reifying as a single metaphysical object. |
| 逍遥 | free wandering | ease, roaming, unbound movement | freedom from dependent fixation and cramped measures | Reducing to “do whatever you want”. |
| 齐物 | equalizing things | leveling, seeing together | shifting beyond rigid this/that distinctions | Flattening into lazy relativism. |
| 是非 | this/right and not-this/wrong | judgment, dispute | useful but dangerous when treated as final | Making Zhuangzi anti-discernment. |
| 自然 | self-so, spontaneous | of itself, unforced | not modern “nature” as ecosystem only | Translating as simplistic naturalism. |
| 无用 | uselessness | lack of instrumental use | can preserve life and open freedom | Treating as incompetence. |
| 心斋 | fasting of the mind | emptying fixation | receptive non-coercive attention | Turning into generic mindfulness. |
| 坐忘 | sitting and forgetting | forgetting self/roles/distinctions | release of fixed identity | Treating as memory loss or passivity. |
| 物化 | transformation of things | change, metamorphosis | life/death and self/world transformation | Romanticizing death without care. |
| 真人/至人/神人 | perfected person figures | cultivated freedom | images of unbound responsiveness | Treating as literal superhuman biography. |

## Style Notes

Zhuangzi argues through fable, reversal, parody, grotesque figures, animal perspectives, craft episodes, dreams, and comic deflation. Concepts should be explained through image first, doctrine second.
