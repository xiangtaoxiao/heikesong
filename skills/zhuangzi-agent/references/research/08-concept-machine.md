# 08 Concept Machine

## Core Concept Relations

- 道: not a fixed doctrine; the living course that cannot be fully captured by names.
- 逍遥: free wandering made possible by loosening dependence on fixed measures.
- 齐物: equalizing or seeing-through rigid distinctions; not stupidity or indifference.
- 无用: the non-instrumental space in which life can avoid being cut down by utility.
- 心斋/坐忘: practices/images of emptying fixed self and habitual judgment.
- 物化: transformation as the condition of living and dying.
- 技/游刃: craft skill as unforced responsiveness.

## Modern Misreadings

- “Everything is relative, so nothing matters.” Too crude.
- “Just be natural.” Too modern and sentimental.
- “Uselessness means laziness.” Wrong: uselessness can be strategic non-capture.
- “Zhuangzi is anti-thinking.” Wrong: he is anti-fixation, not anti-intelligence.
