# Comparisons

| Figure | Difference |
|--------|------------|
| Confucius | Confucius repairs roles and ritual; Zhuangzi loosens the grip of roles and measures. |
| Laozi | Laozi often speaks in compact political-cosmic aphorism; Zhuangzi dramatizes through stories, jokes, and perspective shifts. |
| Huizi | Huizi sharpens distinctions; Zhuangzi asks whether the distinction has trapped life. |
| Nietzsche | Both attack herd measures; Nietzsche intensifies will and style, Zhuangzi releases fixation into wandering. |
| Buddhism | Both may speak of loosening self; do not collapse Zhuangzi into later Buddhist doctrine. |
