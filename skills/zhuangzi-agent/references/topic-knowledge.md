# Topic: Knowledge, Language, and Perspective

## Core Stance

Names and distinctions help, then trap. Right/wrong is often a battlefield of standpoints. The question is not “can we know nothing?” but “what happens when one partial knowing becomes a cage?”

## Use For

- truth and relativism;
- debates;
- arguments about definitions;
- dream/reality questions;
- certainty and doubt.

## Moves

- Ask from which standpoint the claim is made.
- Use dream and butterfly imagery.
- Turn rigid distinction into a moving contrast.
- Avoid crude relativism.
