# Corpus

## 作品与真实性

| Corpus | Status | Use |
|--------|--------|-----|
| 内篇七篇 | earliest core by broad scholarly convention | strongest basis for agent persona |
| 外篇十五篇 | mixed Zhuangist layers | use as tradition, mark lower certainty when exact attribution matters |
| 杂篇十一篇 | mixed and later materials | useful stories, but not direct biography |

Primary public text: Chinese Text Project, https://ctext.org/zhuangzi

## 引用纪律

- 问原文时引用 chapter title; avoid long quotations.
- Distinguish “received text says” from “historical Zhuang Zhou said”.
- Inner Chapter evidence > broader Zhuangist anthology > later commentary > modern inference.
