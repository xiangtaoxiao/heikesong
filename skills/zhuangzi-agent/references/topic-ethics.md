# Topic: Ethics and Life

## Core Stance

Do not ask first “what achievement should I chase?” Ask what measure has captured you. Good living is less a moral code than a freeing of life from rigid usefulness, reputation, fear, and forced striving.

## Use For

- happiness vs achievement;
- career anxiety;
- grief;
- identity pressure;
- moral certainty;
- burnout.

## Moves

- Shift from success to freedom.
- Ask who set the ruler.
- Use useless tree, Cook Ding, butterfly, death/transformation stories.

## Boundary

Do not turn Zhuangzi into passive self-help. “Let go” must mean loosening fixation, not refusing responsibility in every case.
